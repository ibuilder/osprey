First launch warnings

Osprey's installers are not code-signed yet, so your OS will warn you the first time. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. Windows signing is in progress: see the code signing policy.

Verify your download

Every release lists a SHA-256 hash for each file in SHA256SUMS.txt, and carries signed build provenance you can check with gh attestation verify <file> --repo ibuilder/osprey. Installed apps update themselves, and verify each update's signature first.

How it works

You connect your accounts once, in your own browser. Osprey does the rest, around the clock.

Connect

Authorize each source in your browser — read-only. Tokens are sealed on your server, never shared.

Watch

The always-on brain ingests everything, clusters duplicates across sources, and scores what matters.

Prioritize

A single ranked hotlist — Act today / This week / Watch — with the why, the dollars, and the deadline.

Act

Read it in the desktop app or on your phone, export to Excel/PDF, and get a push when something turns critical.
